# Kural Privacy Policy

Kural doesn't collect, store or share any personal data with its developer or with third parties.

**What the app connects to**
- **Your PinePods server.** The server address, username and password you enter are used only to sign in to that server. The resulting API key is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device. Your library, listening history and playback progress are read from and written to your server. That server is operated by you or whoever you chose to trust, not by the Kural developer.
- **Podcast hosts.** Audio files and artwork are downloaded directly from the addresses in your podcasts' feeds, as with any podcast player.

**What stays on your device**
- Downloaded episodes, your queue, playback positions not yet sent to the server, settings, and the automation activity log are stored in the app's own storage and removed when you delete the app.

**No analytics, advertising or tracking.** The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be raised as an issue in the project's repository.

*Last updated: September 2026.*
